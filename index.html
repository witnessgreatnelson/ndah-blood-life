🧠 Myths vs Truth

Myth: “If I donate, I go weak”
Truth: Body replaces fluid in 24 hrs.
Myth: “My blood go finish”
Truth: You donate only 450ml — 1 bottle of water. Body has 5 litres.
Myth: “Donation na for money”
Truth: Real donation is FREE.
Myth: “Only men fit donate”
Truth: Women fit donate too.
